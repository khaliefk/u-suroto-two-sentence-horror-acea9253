## The story

> My daughter's school drawing shows our family at dinner: me, her mother, and the tall man who stands behind my chair, watching us eat. Her teacher called to ask why she drew the tall man with my face.

Exactly two sentences, 200 characters.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`. It is fully deterministic: no randomness, no network, no environment input. It asserts the gate itself before writing: exactly two sentences and 1–400 characters.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
